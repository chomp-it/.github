
Hey! Welcome to Chomp! We're focused on making things easier.

Yeah, I know that sums up just about every organization ever but Chomp takes a different approach.
Our tools aren't general purpose one stop shops. Instead, I pick a field that is painful for no reason and I remove all the pretentious boilerplate.

That can be seen in our products like Hamachi and Kahvi/French Press CoffeeScript. 
Research projects don't have to be hard, and manipulating the DOM shouldn't be either.

We don't really have any true bounds; I go where the useless boilerplate is -- which just so happens to be everywhere.
Therefore, you'll see our projects all over the place and in a billion different fields.

Long story short, Chomp is working on making things easier by questioning the things people have come to accept, and it's been working out pretty well for us.

If you want to take a tour of the organization , I'd recommend starting with Kahvi or Ikura.

Cheers!
